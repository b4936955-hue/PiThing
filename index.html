Search for letters

Pi's digits are read as the numbers 1–26 (A=1 … Z=26). Two digits are taken together when they make 10–26, otherwise one digit stands alone; a lone 0 has no letter. So 3.14159265… reads 3, 14, 15, 9, 26, 5 = C N O I Z E. Letter offsets are digit offsets.
